Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

App: Wonder Dash: Magic World

Developer: WonderDash Studios

Family & Children Friendly Commitment: Wonder Dash: Magic World is designed for general audiences and family play. We are fully committed to protecting the privacy of children and complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not require users to register accounts or submit Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as real names, home addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses to play Wonder Dash: Magic World.

All core gameplay progress, scores, coins, gems, and unlocks are stored locally on your device storage.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game may display family-safe advertisements served by Google AdMob. In compliance with child-directed policies:

  • Ads are configured with TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE.
  • Ad content rating is strictly limited to G (General Audiences).
  • Non-personalized, contextual family-friendly ads are prioritized.
  • No behavioral tracking or interest-based profiling of children is conducted.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Wonder Dash: Magic World offers optional digital in-game items (such as removing advertisements or cosmetic character bundles). All transactions are processed directly and securely through Google Play Billing. We never store, receive, or process your credit card, debit card, or banking details.

4. Data Storage and Offline Play

Player game state (high scores, unlocked worlds, character skins) is stored locally on your device. The core game functions entirely offline without an internet connection.
